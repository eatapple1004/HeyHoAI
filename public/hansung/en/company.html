Hansung Group

Founded in 2007, Hansung Special Vehicles runs four factories and specialises in special-purpose truck bodies and variable axles, producing around 20 product types including wing bodies, refrigerated containers, bulk cement trucks, car carriers and moving floors. Hansung Mobility became its wholly owned subsidiary in 2025.
