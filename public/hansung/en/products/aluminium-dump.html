Aluminium Dump Trailer

A dump trailer with a body sized to your cargo. Tilt warning and roll stability support keep tipping safe.

47m³Largest body volume (aluminium)

Configuration

  • Aluminium frame for lower tare weight
  • Bodies: steel 18/32/40 m³, aluminium 25/38/43/47 m³
  • SSAB Hardox wear plate
  • Tilting Warning for safe tipping
  • RSS roll stability system with trailer-linked EBS
  • Optional liner for faster discharge
Steel body18 / 32 / 40 m³
Aluminium body25 / 38 / 43 / 47 m³
