ADAM Company Inc. (주식회사아담컴퍼니) (hereinafter the ‘Company’) operates the artificial intelligence marketing content generation service ‘Doppia’ (https://doppia.ai, hereinafter the ‘Service’). To protect the freedoms and rights of data subjects, the Company processes personal information lawfully and manages it securely in compliance with the Personal Information Protection Act (개인정보 보호법) and other applicable laws and regulations. Pursuant to Article 30 of the Personal Information Protection Act (개인정보 보호법), the Company establishes and discloses this Privacy Policy in order to inform data subjects of the procedures and standards governing the processing of their personal information and to enable the prompt and smooth handling of related complaints and grievances.

The core function of this Service is to process facial photographs (selfies) and product photographs uploaded by users through artificial intelligence in order to generate photographs, videos, and advertising content. In the course of this processing, uploaded images and input text (prompts) are transferred to and processed by overseas artificial intelligence processing subcontractors. The Company transparently discloses such processing in Article 5 (Entrustment of Processing), Article 6 (Cross-Border Transfers), and Article 7 (Processing of Facial Images and Similar Data) of this Policy.

Article 1 (Items of Personal Information Collected and Methods of Collection)

The Company collects the following personal information in the course of membership registration, use of the Service, content generation, payment, and related processes.

1. Membership Registration and Account Management

  • Required: Email address, password (stored with one-way encryption; the Company does not retain plaintext passwords)
  • Optional: Name or nickname (display name)
  • When using social login (Google): Google account email, name, profile picture URL, and Google account identifier (unique ID). The Company receives information within the ‘openid, email, profile’ scopes from Google.
  • Business/trial accounts: Company name (limited to accounts issued by an administrator)

2. Information Directly Uploaded by Users in the Course of Content Generation

  • Facial photographs (selfies) and images of persons: Facial images uploaded by users for the generation of AI photographs and videos of the same person (up to 14 images per single generation, plus a representative image for character registration). Facial images constitute personal visual information; the Company processes them solely for the purpose of generating the content requested by the user, and not for purposes of identity verification or identification (biometric recognition). By registering for membership and uploading images, users confirm the contents of this Policy and consent to such processing.
  • Product photographs: Product images uploaded for the generation of advertising and UGC scripts and content
  • Input text: Prompts, advertising concepts, scripts, and other text entered by users

3. Information Generated in the Course of Service Use and Content Generation

  • AI-generated outputs (images, videos, advertising scripts), and public/private visibility settings for the creation gallery
  • Credit accrual and usage records (credit ledger), and referral/affiliate relationship information

4. Information Collected in the Course of Payment

  • Order number, product (credit pack/pass) information, payment amount, payment authorization result, and purchaser identification information (email, etc.)
  • Payment instrument details such as card number, expiration date, and CVC are neither collected nor retained by the Company; they are processed and retained by the electronic payment gateway providers (PG companies). The Company retains only the order number, payment result, and authorization token.

5. Information Automatically Collected or Generated in the Course of Service Use

  • IP address, access date and time, device and browser information (User-Agent), Service usage records, and error logs
  • Cookies (authentication cookie, referral cookie, and social login security (state) cookie) — see Article 10 for details

Methods of Collection

  • Direct input by users during membership registration, Service use, content upload and generation, and payment
  • Provision by Google upon linking Google social login
  • Automatic collection through generation tool (web) and infrastructure (server and cloud) logs

Article 2 (Purposes of Processing Personal Information)

The Company uses the collected personal information solely for the following purposes, and where any purpose changes, the Company will take necessary measures, including obtaining separate consent, in accordance with Article 18 of the Personal Information Protection Act (개인정보 보호법).

  • Member management: Identification and authentication of members in connection with the use of the membership-based Service, maintenance and management of membership status, verification of duplicate registration, prevention of fraudulent use, and delivery of notices
  • Service provision (core): Generation and provision of AI photographs, videos, and advertising content based on facial and product photographs and input text uploaded by users; regeneration of the same person or product; and provision of the creation gallery
  • Payment and settlement: Payment processing for purchases of credit packs and passes; granting, deduction, and refund of credits (restoration of credits upon generation failure); purchase history inquiries; settlement of referral commissions; and prevention of fraudulent or duplicate payments
  • Customer support: Handling of inquiries and complaints, retention of records for dispute resolution, and delivery of announcements
  • Service improvement and security: Ensuring Service stability, analysis of access frequency, detection and prevention of fraudulent use, and incident response

Article 7 (Processing of Sensitive Personal Information Such as Facial Images)

The core function of this Service is to generate AI photographs and videos of the same person based on facial photographs uploaded by users. As facial images constitute personal visual information contained in photographs and videos and are capable of identifying a specific individual, the Company manages them with particular care as follows.

  • Identity confirmation and consent: When uploading a facial image, the user must confirm that the face or content is the user's own or that the user holds legitimate rights or consent with respect thereto, and the Company provides a confirmation (consent) procedure for this purpose. Uploading another person's face without legitimate consent is prohibited, and the responsibility for doing so rests with the uploading user.
  • Limitation of purpose of use: Uploaded facial images are used solely for the generation and regeneration of content requested by the user, and are not used for any other purpose such as advertising, marketing, or model training.
  • Cross-border transfer: Facial images are transferred to overseas processing subcontractors, such as Google (United States) and Kling (China), for content generation as specified in Article 6. This constitutes the entrustment of personal information processing necessary for the provision of the service requested by the user (performance of the service agreement), and the Company discloses its details in this Policy pursuant to Article 28-8, Paragraph 1, Item 3 of the Personal Information Protection Act (개인정보 보호법). Users who do not wish their information to be transferred overseas may refrain from using the facial-image-based generation features.
  • Destruction: When a user deletes a character or creation or withdraws from membership, the related facial images are destroyed.

Article 8 (Procedures and Methods for Destruction of Personal Information)

The Company destroys personal information without delay when the information becomes unnecessary, such as upon the expiration of the retention period or the achievement of the purpose of processing.

  • Destruction procedure: When a user requests membership withdrawal or similar, the Company designates the relevant information for destruction; information that must be retained under applicable laws is moved to a separate database (DB) or separate storage location and destroyed after the retention period expires. Information stored separately in this manner is not used for any other purpose except as provided by law.
  • Destruction method: Information in electronic file form is deleted using technical methods that render the records irrecoverable, and personal information recorded on paper documents is destroyed by shredding or incineration.
  • Membership withdrawal processing: Upon membership withdrawal, the Company deactivates the account so that login and reuse are impossible, and then, as a rule, destroys without delay any personal information not subject to statutory retention obligations.

Article 10 (Measures to Ensure the Security of Personal Information)

In accordance with Article 29 of the Personal Information Protection Act (개인정보 보호법), the Company implements the following technical, administrative, and physical measures necessary to ensure security.

  • Password encryption: User passwords are stored and managed using one-way encryption (hashing), and the Company does not retain plaintext passwords.
  • Encryption in transit: Access to the Service and data transmissions to overseas processing subcontractors are encrypted using security protocols such as SSL/TLS.
  • Separation of payment information: Payment instrument details such as card numbers are not retained by the Company and are processed by certified electronic payment gateway providers.
  • Access authority management: Access to personal information is limited to the minimum necessary personnel, and access rights are managed through granting, modification, and revocation.
  • Retention of access records: The Company retains and manages access records of its personal information processing systems.
  • Infrastructure security: Data is stored and managed in the secure cloud environment of Amazon Web Services (Seoul Region), and security systems are operated to prevent external intrusion.

Article 11 (Installation, Operation, and Refusal of Automatic Personal Information Collection Devices — Cookies)

The Company uses cookies to keep users logged in and to provide personalized services.

  • Authentication cookie (token): Used to maintain login sessions and for API authentication; secured (httpOnly) and retained for approximately 7 days.
  • Referral cookie (ref): Used to identify sign-up relationships through referral links; retained for approximately 60 days.
  • Social login security cookie (state): Used for security (CSRF prevention) during the social login process; retained for approximately 10 minutes.

Users may refuse the storage of cookies or delete them through their web browser settings. However, refusing the storage of the authentication cookie may restrict the use of services that require login.
