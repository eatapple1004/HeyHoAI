Article 1 (Nature of Products and Goods Sold)

All goods provided and sold by the Company through the Service constitute digital content under the Content Industry Promotion Act (콘텐츠산업진흥법) and services provided through information and communications networks; no tangible goods requiring physical delivery are sold.

  • Credits (◈): A prepaid usage unit (usage allowance) used within the Service to generate AI photos, videos, and advertising content. Credits are not cash, are not redeemable or refundable for cash, and may be used solely for purposes within the Service. The service provision period (validity period) of Credits is 12 months from the date of purchase (payment), and any Credits not used within the validity period shall expire upon its lapse. In addition to paid purchases (Credit Packs), Credits may also be granted free of charge, such as sign-up bonuses, attendance bonuses, and referral rewards; such free-of-charge Credits are excluded from refunds.
  • Subscription Plans (Passes): Period-based passes that provide tier-specific feature access and Credits for a fixed period.
  • AI-Generated Outputs: Digital content, such as images, videos, and advertising scripts, that the user generates and receives immediately by using Credits.

The withdrawal-of-subscription and refund policies set forth in this Policy are premised on the characteristics of such digital content and services (provision commences immediately upon purchase; consumption and impairment of value occur upon use).

Article 2 (Principles and Period of Withdrawal of Subscription)

With respect to paid payments (purchase of a Credit Pack or payment for a Subscription Plan), the user may withdraw the subscription within the following periods.

  • Within 7 days from the date the user receives a written document regarding the terms of the contract (including electronic documents, such as payment completion notices and receipts) or from the date the supply of the goods or services commenced (Article 17(1) of the E-Commerce Act).
  • If the content of the goods, etc. differs from what was displayed or advertised, or the contract was performed in a manner different from its terms, the user may withdraw the subscription within 3 months from the date of receiving the goods, or within 30 days from the date the user became aware (or could have become aware) of such fact (Article 17(3) of the E-Commerce Act).

Provided, however, that withdrawal of subscription may be restricted where any of the grounds for restriction set forth in Article 3 applies.

Article 3 (Restrictions on Withdrawal of Subscription for Digital Content)

Pursuant to Article 17(2)5 of the E-Commerce Act, the user's right to withdraw the subscription is restricted where the provision of services or digital content has commenced. However, for divisible services or digital content, withdrawal of subscription is available with respect to the portion for which provision has not yet commenced.

The commencement of provision and consumption for each type of goods in the Service is as follows. The Company displays these facts on the payment screen so that the user can easily recognize them, and proceeds with payment only after obtaining the user's prior consent (consent to the restriction of the right of withdrawal) (Article 17(6) of the E-Commerce Act).

  • Credit Packs: Upon completion of payment, Credits (usage allowance) are immediately granted to the user's account, whereby provision of the goods commences. Any portion of the granted Credits that has been used (consumed) at least once for content generation is impaired in value, and withdrawal of subscription is restricted for such portion. Credits are deducted and consumed from the account balance immediately before a generation request, and consumed Credits cannot be restored to their original state once consumption is finalized.
  • AI-Generated Outputs (images, videos, advertising scripts, etc.): Because the AI produces content simultaneously with the user's generation request and the content becomes immediately available for delivery and download, each completed item of content is deemed to have commenced provision, and withdrawal of subscription is restricted for it.
  • Subscription Plans (Passes): Because usage rights are activated immediately upon payment and the Credits for the applicable period are granted in a lump sum, the provision of services commences at that time; accordingly, withdrawal of subscription is restricted after use has commenced (the mid-term cancellation and refund provisions of Article 5 apply).

Accordingly, withdrawal of subscription (full refund) for the unused portion is available only where the user has not used any Credits after purchase and the withdrawal period under Article 2 has not lapsed; portions already consumed or used, and content whose generation has been completed, are excluded from withdrawal of subscription.

Article 4 (Credit Pack Refund Policy)

Refunds for one-time Credit Packs are governed by the following standards. Because Credits are consumable and irreversible goods, used (consumed) Credits are not refundable.

  • Full refund for unused Credits: If the request is made within 7 days from the payment date and the purchased Credits have not been used even once, the full amount of the relevant payment will be refunded.
  • Partial use: If part of the purchased Credits has been used, withdrawal of subscription is restricted with respect to the portion for which provision has commenced and which has been consumed. In accordance with applicable laws and the purpose of consumer protection, the Company may refund an amount corresponding to the unused remaining Credits where there is a cause attributable to the Company, such as a clear defect in the Service. In such cases, the refund amount is calculated as follows.
    Refund amount = payment amount × (number of unused Credits ÷ total number of purchased Credits). Provided, that bonus Credits granted free of charge are excluded from the total number of purchased Credits and from the refund calculation.
  • Credit restoration upon generation failure: If content generation technically fails due to causes on the Company's side, such as system or network errors, the Credits for the failed generation will be automatically restored (credited back) to the account balance. This is a restoration of the Credit balance, not a monetary refund, and the user may use the restored Credits for regeneration without any separate request.
  • Free-of-charge Credits: Credits granted free of charge, such as sign-up bonuses, attendance bonuses, and referral rewards, do not constitute paid purchase consideration and are therefore not subject to refund (return of cash).

The Credit Packs actually sold by the Company and the payment currencies are as displayed on the payment screen and the pricing page within the Service. The validity period (service provision period) of Credits is 12 months from the date of purchase (payment), and any Credits not used within that period shall expire upon its lapse. Detailed standards, such as extension of validity periods and advance notice of impending expiration, are as set forth on the payment screen and in the Terms of Service.

Article 5 (Auto-Renewal, Cancellation, and Mid-Term Cancellation Refunds for Subscription Plans)

1. Auto-Renewal and Notice of Billing Date

Where recurring billing (auto-renewal) applies to a Subscription Plan, the Company clearly discloses on the payment screen the billing cycle, the amount charged at each renewal, the next scheduled billing date, and whether auto-renewal applies, and proceeds with payment only after obtaining the user's consent. The user may check the next scheduled billing date and the current subscription status at any time on the "Billing/Subscription Management" screen within the Service.

2. Cancellation Method and Timing

  • The user may request cancellation of the subscription at any time through the "Billing/Subscription Management" screen within the Service or through the Customer Center (support@doppia.ai, 070-8098-3546).
  • If the user requests cancellation before the next billing date, the next billing cycle will not be charged, and the user may continue to use the Service at the applicable tier until the already-paid period expires (the subscription terminates automatically upon expiration of the period).
  • If a cancellation request is received close to the next billing date and the payment has already been processed, the relevant payment is governed by the "Mid-Term Cancellation Refunds" standards below.

3. Mid-Term Cancellation Refunds

Where the user cancels in the middle of a subscription period after use has commenced, withdrawal of subscription is restricted with respect to services whose provision has already commenced; however, the Company processes refunds for the remaining period in accordance with the following standards.

  • Before commencement of use, within 7 days: If the request is made within 7 days after payment and none of the granted Credits has been used, the full amount will be refunded.
  • After commencement of use: The Company refunds the balance of the payment amount after deducting (the amount corresponding to the number of days of use already elapsed) and (the amount corresponding to the Credits already used). The pro-rata formula is as follows.
    Refund amount = payment amount − (payment amount × elapsed days ÷ total days of the period) − amount corresponding to paid Credits already used. If the calculated result is negative, the refund amount shall be KRW 0.
  • Where an annual (prepaid) subscription is cancelled mid-term, the pro-rata calculation above applies mutatis mutandis, and where the Company has granted a discount benefit, the used portion may be settled based on the regular price.

※ The Service's recurring subscription billing feature is being rolled out in stages subject to merchant review procedures, and the auto-renewal and cancellation provisions of this Article apply in full from the point at which recurring billing (auto-renewal) actually commences. Where the Service is provided as a prepaid period-based pass before recurring billing commences, Article 3 and Paragraph 3 of this Article (Mid-Term Cancellation Refunds) apply.

  • Payment errors and duplicate payments: If a payment is made contrary to the user's actual intent, or the same transaction is charged more than once, due to system errors, communication failures, payment gateway (PG) processing errors, or the like, the user may notify the Customer Center, and after verifying the facts, the Company will cancel and refund the full amount of the erroneous or duplicate payment without delay. The Company applies idempotent processing to prevent duplicate payments; if a duplicate payment is nonetheless confirmed, the Company will refund the excess payment regardless of whether the goods have been used.
  • Payments by minors: If a minor under the age of 19 makes a payment without the consent of his or her legal representative, the minor or the legal representative may cancel the payment pursuant to Article 5 of the Civil Act (민법). However, this shall not apply where cancellation is restricted by law, such as where the minor used deception to cause the Company to believe that he or she was an adult or that the legal representative's consent had been obtained, or where the payment was made within the scope of property the legal representative permitted the minor to dispose of. Upon a cancellation request, the Company may request documents verifying the legal-representative relationship and the minor status.
