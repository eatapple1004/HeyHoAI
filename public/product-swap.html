How it works

1Upload your video

A 3–15 second clip where a product is clearly visible — held, on a table, in use.

2Add your product

One clear photo of your product. Say what to replace if there is more than one object.

3Swap

Same motion, new product — lit with the scene's light. Open it in the Video Editor to trim, add text or music.

Works best when the original and your product are a similar size and shape (bottle → bottle, box → box). Longer videos: pick the 15-second part to use. Credits are refunded if the swap fails.
