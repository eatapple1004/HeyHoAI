FAQ

Before you start — the questions everyone asks

Can I really start for free?

Yes. You get 1,500 credits the moment you sign up — no card required, cancel anytime. Make something first, then decide.

Will it work for what I sell?

Whatever you sell, yes. From one product photo we create detail cuts, on-model shots, editorials and ad videos — any category.

What do I need to prepare?

Just one product photo — no extra angles, no references. For ad videos, add one line about your product, or leave it blank and Doppia writes it from the photo.

Can I use the images commercially?

Yes — every plan, including Free, comes with commercial use. Product pages, ads, social — anywhere.

How are credits charged?

Credits are spent per generation, and failed generations refund automatically — you only pay for what comes out. Subscription credits refill monthly; top-ups stay valid for 12 months.

Subscription vs top-up — what's the difference?

Subscriptions refill credits automatically every month with all features open. Top-ups need no subscription — add credits when you want, valid for 12 months. Mixing both works great.
