FAQ

Before you spend a credit

Will this work for what I sell?

Today Doppia is built for three things: apparel, jewelry and cosmetics. If that's what you sell, the looks are ready. If you sell food, electronics, furniture or pet — we're not there yet. We'd rather tell you now than take your signup.

What do I have to give it?

One product photo. That's the whole input — no second angle, no reference sheet. For an ad video, add one line about the product, or let Doppia read the photo and draft that line for you. More angles help: an ad video reads up to five photos of the same product together.

What does a credit actually buy?

A template photo is ◆300 and every run returns four. A custom-prompt photo is ◆150. An ad video is ◆625 per five-second scene — a 20-second ad is four scenes, ◆2,500. You only pay for what renders.

What happens to my photos?

Your files sit on AWS Seoul. From there, Shots go to a single vendor — Google, in the US. Ad Video uses four: Claude for the script, Gemini for the scenes, ElevenLabs for the score, and Kling for the motion — the first three in the US, Kling in China. Every vendor is named in our privacy policy with its country, what we send it, and its privacy contact. Doppia doesn't train on your uploads. Read the vendor table →
